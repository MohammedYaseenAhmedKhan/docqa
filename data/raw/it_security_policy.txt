IT Security Policy

Password Policy:
Employees must not share passwords with anyone.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and follow complexity rules.

Device Usage:
Company-issued laptops must be used for official work only.
Personal devices are not allowed to access company systems without approval.

Remote Access and VPN:
VPN access is mandatory when working from outside the office network.
Employees must connect to the company VPN before accessing internal tools.

Software Installation:
Installing unauthorized software is strictly prohibited.
All software requests must be approved by the IT department.
