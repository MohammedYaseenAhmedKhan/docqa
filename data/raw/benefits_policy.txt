Employee Benefits Policy

Health Insurance:
All full-time employees are covered under the company health insurance plan.
Coverage includes hospitalization and basic medical expenses.

Provident Fund:
The company contributes to the employee provident fund as per government regulations.

Bonus Policy:
Annual performance-based bonuses are awarded based on individual and company performance.

Reimbursements:
Employees may claim reimbursements for approved travel, meals, and training expenses.
All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days.
