Company Overview

ABC Technologies is a mid-sized software services company operating in India.
The company provides software development, data analytics, and IT consulting services.

Departments:
- Engineering
- Product Management
- Human Resources
- Sales and Marketing
- IT Support

Working Hours:
Office hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees are expected to be available during core working hours from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Remote Work:
Remote or hybrid work is allowed with prior approval from the reporting manager.
Employees working remotely must follow IT security guidelines.
