Employee Onboarding Guide

First Day Checklist:
- Report to HR for document verification
- Collect employee ID card
- Receive laptop and system access

System Access:
Employees must raise an IT ticket to request email and tool access.
Access is typically granted within 1 working day.

HR Support:
For HR-related queries, employees can contact the HR team via email or the HR portal.

IT Support:
For technical issues, employees should contact the IT helpdesk.
Critical issues should be reported immediately.
