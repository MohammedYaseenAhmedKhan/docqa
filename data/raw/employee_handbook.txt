Employee Handbook

Leave Policy:
Employees must request leave at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.
Manager approval is mandatory for all leave requests.

Types of Leave:
- Casual Leave: 12 days per calendar year
- Sick Leave: Medical certificate required if leave exceeds 2 consecutive days
- Earned Leave: Accumulated monthly and can be carried forward

Attendance Policy:
Employees must mark daily attendance using the HR system.
More than 3 late arrivals in a month may require an explanation to HR.

Code of Conduct:
Employees must maintain professional behavior at the workplace.
Harassment, discrimination, or misconduct will result in disciplinary action.
